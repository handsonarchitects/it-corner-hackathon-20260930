# Running costs

Pyrlandia Dostawy (a fictional company), figures from finance and the depot managers,
September 2026. All amounts in PLN, net of VAT.

## Diesel

- Diesel price: 5.20 PLN per litre (our average fuel card price over the last three months).
- Average fuel use per model, from fuel card data:

| Diesel model | Fuel use | Rated payload |
|--------------|----------|---------------|
| Brona D35 | 9.6 L/100 km | 1,150 kg |
| Brona D35 Long | 10.9 L/100 km | 1,050 kg |
| Kestrel Cargo 3.5 | 11.8 L/100 km | 1,300 kg |

## Maintenance

| Van type | Maintenance cost |
|----------|------------------|
| Diesel van | 0.34 PLN per km |
| EV van (dealer estimate) | 0.14 PLN per km |

Maintenance covers servicing, tyres and repairs. Insurance costs the same for both types.
