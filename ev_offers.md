# EV van offers

Offer from the dealer for Pyrlandia Dostawy (fictional), valid until 31 Oct 2026.
Volta Cargo is a fictional EV van brand.

All prices in PLN, net of VAT.

| Model | Range (WLTP) | Payload | Energy use | Purchase price | Lease |
|-------|--------------|---------|------------|----------------|-------|
| Volta Cargo S | 260 km | 1,050 kg | 24 kWh/100 km | 150,000 PLN | 2,900 PLN/month |
| Volta Cargo L | 380 km | 880 kg | 27 kWh/100 km | 195,000 PLN | 3,770 PLN/month |

## Notes from the dealer

- **Range** is the WLTP combined figure from the type approval.
- **Energy use** is the dealer's figure for city delivery work, measured at the charger.
- **Payload** is the rated payload with a driver on board.
- **Lease:** 60 monthly payments, no deposit, no buyout at the end (the van goes back to the
  dealer). Maintenance and energy are not included. The monthly payment is fixed for the
  whole term.
- **Purchase:** price per van, delivery within 8 weeks of the order. Same price for any
  number of vans up to 12.
- Both models charge from a 22 kW AC point; a full charge takes under 5 hours on
  Volta Cargo S and under 7 hours on Volta Cargo L.
