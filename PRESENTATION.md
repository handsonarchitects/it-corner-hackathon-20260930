# Presentation guide

About 15 minutes per team: process first, short demo last.

## What to cover

1. **Your workflow.** A high-level walk from the data Ewa sent you to the software you ended up with.
2. **Humans and agents.** What role each person had and how they worked with the agents. Where did people put their time (e.g. analysing the data, preparing it, writing prompts, reviewing agent output, testing)?
3. **Tools and technologies.** The agents, models, editors, languages and anything else you used today.
4. **Your usual way of working.** Where did your usual way of working not fit today?
5. **What you'll do differently.** With what you know after today, name one thing you will do differently as a team, and one thing you would change in how you worked today.
6. **Demo.** Rerun your tool on the latest export.

## Send us your answers

Before your presentation, put your answers to points 1-5 in one Markdown file, `<team-name>.md`, and email it to both judges:

- maciej@handsonarchitects.com
- tomek@handsonarchitects.com
